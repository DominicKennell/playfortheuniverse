How to play
  • You and your opponent each have a pack of 15 cards: values 1 to 5 in Wood, Rock and Metal. You hold 5 cards.
  • Each round, pick a card and press Play. Your opponent picks at the same time, then both cards flip.
  • Wood beats Rock, Rock beats Metal, Metal beats Wood. Same family: the lower value wins. The smallest are the mightiest!
  • The winner takes the losing card as a trophy and uses their card's ability. Your winning card goes to your discard pile.
  • Same family and same value is a draw. Both cards wait in the middle, and the next round is a Showdown: no abilities, and the winner takes everything in the middle too.
  • After 5 rounds, the highest trophy total wins. A tie, or a draw in round 5, means sudden death.
  • First game? Turn Abilities off. Turn them on once you know how to play.
